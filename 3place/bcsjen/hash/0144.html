0003 avail

Pronunciation

/əˈveɪl/ (uh-VAYL)

Etymology & Root

  • Old French avoir “to have” (from Latin habere “to have”).

English-to-English explanation

To make use of something; to be useful or beneficial.

Example:

The gendarmerie were sent after him, but to no avail.
