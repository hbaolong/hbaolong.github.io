XI Christus Nos Liberavit

单词故事：Christus

  • Christus,Christus 是拉丁语中对耶稣基督的称呼，核心含义是“受膏者”，也就是希伯来语中的“弥赛亚”（Messiah）。

  • liberavit,LIBERAVIT 是拉丁语动词 liberare（释放、使自由）的第三人称单数完成时主动直陈式，意思是：“他释放了 / 他已使……自由了。”

What is this story of Fantine about? It is about society buying a slave.

From whom? From wretchedness.

  • wretchedness,英 [ˈretʃɪdnəs] 美 [ˈretʃɪdnəs] n. 可怜；悲惨；不幸

From hunger, cold, isolation, neglect, destitution. A hard bargain. A soul for a morsel of bread. Society accepts what wretchedness offers.

  • hunger,英 [ˈhʌŋɡə(r)] 美 [ˈhʌŋɡər] n. 饥饿感；饥饿，饥荒；渴求，渴望 v. 渴求，渴望；挨饿

  • destitution,英 [ˌdestɪˈtjuːʃ(ə)n] 美 [ˌdestɪˈtuːʃ(ə)n] n. 穷困；缺乏

  • bargain,英 [ˈbɑːɡən] 美 [ˈbɑːrɡən] n. 便宜货，减价品；协议，交易 v. 讨价还价，谈判

  • morsel,英 [ˈmɔːs(ə)l] 美 [ˈmɔːrs(ə)l] n. 一口；（食物）少量

The sacred law of Jesus Christ governs our civilization but does not yet pervade it. It is said that slavery has disappeared from European civilization. This is false. It still exists, but only women suffer this oppression now, and its name is prostitution.

  • sacred,英 [ˈseɪkrɪd] 美 [ˈseɪkrɪd] adj. 神的，神圣的；宗教的，宗教性的；受尊重的，受崇敬的；不可侵犯的，不容干涉的

  • pervade,英 [pəˈveɪd] 美 [pərˈveɪd] vt. <正式>遍及，弥漫

  • oppression,英 [əˈpreʃn] 美 [əˈpreʃn] n. 压迫，压制；压迫手段；郁闷，压抑

  • prostitution,英 [ˌprɒstɪˈtjuːʃ(ə)n] 美 [ˌprɑːstɪˈtuːʃ(ə)n] n. 卖淫；滥用；出卖灵魂

It oppresses women, that is to say feminine charm, weakness, beauty, motherhood. This is not one of the least of man’s reasons to be ashamed.

  • oppress,英 [əˈpres] 美 [əˈpres] v. 压迫，欺压；使压抑，使烦恼；（纹章）使（图记）部分模糊

  • feminine,英 [ˈfemənɪn] 美 [ˈfemənɪn] adj. 女性特有的，女子气的；女性的，妇女的；（语法）阴性的 n. （语法）阴性词，阴性形式

At the point we have now reached in this painful tragedy, there is nothing left of the Fantine she once was. Sinking into the mire, she has hardened into stone. Anyone who touches her feels cold. She passes by, enduring you, ignoring you. She is the stern figure of disgrace. Life and the social order have said all they have to say to her. Everything that will happen to her has happened. She has been through everything, borne everything, sustained everything, suffered everything, lost everything, mourned everything. She is resigned with a resignation that resembles indifference as death resembles sleep. There is nothing more she shrinks from, nothing more she fears. Let the whole rain-cloud come down on her and the entire ocean sweep over her! What does she care? She is a saturated sponge.

  • tragedy,英 [ˈtrædʒədi] 美 [ˈtrædʒədi] n. 悲惨事件，惨剧；悲剧（作品）；不幸，遗憾；令人非常烦乱（或生气）的情形

  • mire,英 [ˈmaɪə(r)] 美 [ˈmaɪər] n. 泥潭；[地理]泥沼 vi. 陷于泥坑；陷入困境 vt. 使陷于泥坑；使陷入困境

This at least is what she believes, but it is a mistake to imagine that fate has no more in store and that there is a limit to everything.

Alas! What are all these lives, driven on in such confusion? Where are they going? Why are they like this?

He who knows this sees the full extent of the darkness.

There is none but he. His name is God.
