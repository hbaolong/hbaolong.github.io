20 October The Efficiency of the Profit Motive

  • efficiency,英 [ɪˈfɪʃ(ə)nsi] 美 [ɪˈfɪʃ(ə)nsi] n. 效率，效能；（机器的）功率

  • motive,英 [ˈməʊtɪv] 美 [ˈmoʊtɪv] n. 动机，目的，原因；（文艺作品、音乐的）艺术思想，主题 adj. 引起运动的，起推动作用的 v. 使产生动机，激起

  • profit,英 [ˈprɒfɪt] 美 [ˈprɑːfɪt] n. 利润，盈利；<正式> 利益，好处 v. 获益，得益（于）；赢利，获利

The profit motive alone gives fulfillment through power over things.

  • fulfillment,英 [fʊlˈfɪlmənt] 美 [fʊlˈfɪlmənt] n. 履行；实行；满足（感）

The only relevant and meaningful question is whether the profit motive is the socially most efficient one of the available directions in which the drive for power can be channeled. But we can say that of the channels available and known to us, the profit motive has a very high, if not the highest, social efficiency.

  • relevant,英 [ˈreləvənt] 美 [ˈreləvənt] adj. 有关的，切题的；正确的，适宜的；有价值的，有意义的

  • channel,英 [ˈtʃæn(ə)l] 美 [ˈtʃæn(ə)l] n. 频道，电视频道；管道，通道，航道；渠道，手段；沟渠，沟槽；河床；海峡；信道；电子沟道；输液道 v. 引导，把……用于；输送，传送；通灵；努力赶上，受启发；形成凹槽

All the other known forms in which the lust for power can be expressed offer satisfaction by giving the ambitious man direct power and domination over his fellow men. The profit motive alone gives fulfillment through power over things.

  • lust,英 [lʌst] 美 [lʌst] n. 性欲；强烈欲望；（神学，被看作是罪过的）感官欲望 v. 有强烈的性欲；渴望

ACTION POINT: Take a position: power over things is less dangerous socially than power over people.
