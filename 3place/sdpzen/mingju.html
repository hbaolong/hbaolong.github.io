PREFACE

BOOK ONE

  1. It was a matter of sustenance of the spirit to have on one’s shelves the works of a man with great charm, originality, and integrity of purpose, an enfant terrible, a great original mind that could not conform.

  2. A vivid personality is always an enigma. There had to be one Su Tungpo, but there could not be two.

Chapter One LITERARY PATRIOTIC DUKE

  1. The drunkard reforms, the saint falls, and the pastor runs away with a choir girl.

  2. The question of knowing or not knowing a man has nothing to do with being his contemporary. It is a matter of sympathetic understanding.

  3. one knows only those whom one really understands, and one completely understands only those whom one really likes.

  4. The question of liking a poet is always a question of taste.

  5. I think Li Po reached a greater height of sublimity and Tu Fu reached a greater stature in his total impression as a poet great by all the standards of greatness in poetry—freshness, naturalness, technical skill, and compassions. But without any apology, my favorite poet is Su Tungpo.
