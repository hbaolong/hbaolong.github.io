🏋️‍♂️ Part 3: The Main Workout

1. Hack Squat OR Leg Press (Choose ONE)

  • Weight: 40kg - 45kg

  • Sets / Reps: 3 sets × 10 reps

  • Coach’s Focus: Brace your core tightly. Keep knees tracking over toes. Do NOT round your lower back (strictly protect your right posterior waist).

  • toe,英 [təʊ] 美 [toʊ] n. （人的）脚趾，（兽、鸟的）趾

  • posterior,英 [pɒˈstɪəriə(r)] 美 [pɑːˈstɪriər] adj. 其次的；较后的 n. 后部；臀部

  • waist,英 [weɪst] 美 [weɪst] n. 腰，腰围；（衣服的）腰部，腰身；船腰，飞机机身中部，（提琴、沙漏等的）中间细部，（黄蜂等的）细腰；<美>女衬衫，紧身胸衣

3. Seated Row

  • Weight: 70kg

  • Sets / Reps: 3 sets × 10-12 reps

  • Coach’s Focus: Chest up, core braced. Absolutely NO leaning back to use momentum. Pull strictly with your back and retract your shoulder blades.

  • momentum,英 [məˈmentəm] 美 [moʊˈmentəm] n. 冲力，推力；动力，势头；动量，冲量

  • retract,英 [rɪˈtrækt] 美 [rɪˈtrækt] vt. 缩回；缩进；取消 vi. 缩回；缩进；撤消 n. 收缩核

  • Weight: 30kg

  • Sets / Reps: 2-3 sets × 10 reps

  • Coach’s Focus: Depress and retract your scapula (shoulders down and pinned to the pad). Push until arms are slightly bent (do not lock out elbows), and control the weight on the way back.

6. Core Finisher (5 minutes)

  • Dead Bug: 2 sets × 8 reps per side

  • Bird Dog: 2 sets × 6 reps per side

  • Coach’s Focus: Keep your lower back glued to the floor (Dead Bug) and your pelvis stable (Bird Dog). Move slowly.
